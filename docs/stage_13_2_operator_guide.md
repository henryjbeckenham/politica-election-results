# Stage 13.2 operator guide

Start Politica and open `http://127.0.0.1:8765/results/`, then choose **House**.

Use the state selector or search box to narrow the 150 electorate cards. Select a card to view:

- the declared member and party;
- Retained, Gained from… or New member status;
- TCP margin and candidate bars;
- TPP bars where reported;
- expandable primary votes;
- enrolment, counted votes, turnout and informality.

The application deliberately avoids claiming an open-seat party gain or retention without a governed previous-seat baseline. Stage 13.4 adds national and state analytical House visualisations. It can display AEC-reported TCP swing as supplied, while an independently recalculated historical comparison remains blocked until a compatible earlier election and redistribution policy are registered.
