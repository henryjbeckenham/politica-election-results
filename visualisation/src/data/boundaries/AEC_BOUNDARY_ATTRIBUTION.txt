Politica Election Results: 2022 and 2025 federal electorate boundaries

Politica uses the national AEC federal electoral boundary datasets identified
by the AEC as applying to the 2022 and 2025 federal elections. The respective
sources are the 29 September 2021 release containing 151 divisions and the
4 March 2025 release containing 150 divisions. The web geometries are
simplified for display. The AEC has not evaluated the data as simplified and
incorporated into Politica and gives no warranty regarding its accuracy,
completeness, currency or suitability for any particular purpose.
